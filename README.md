## 🌌 01. Mission Overview

Humanity's future in space depends on more than reaching another planet. It depends on our ability to survive, adapt, and build sustainable habitats in environments far beyond Earth.

**AstroBase** is an interactive Moon and Mars survival simulator developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

Players take on the role of astronauts responsible for operating an extraterrestrial outpost. Every mission requires careful resource management, strategic decision-making, and the ability to respond to unexpected environmental and operational challenges.

Through third-person astronaut exploration, interactive 3D environments, mission progression, and an AI-powered companion, AstroBase transforms complex space survival concepts into an engaging educational simulation.

> **Mission Objective:** Establish, operate, and sustain an off-world outpost while keeping essential life-support resources and mission objectives under control.

## 🎮 02. Mission Systems & Key Features

AstroBase combines interactive gameplay with concepts inspired by real-world space exploration and human spaceflight challenges.

- 🚀 **3D Astronaut Operations** — Explore planetary outposts through third-person astronaut movement.
- 🌕 **Lunar Survival Campaign** — Operate a Moon outpost through a 20-day mission.
- 🔴 **Martian Survival Campaign** — Manage a more demanding 30-day Mars mission.
- ⚡ **Life-Support Resource Management** — Monitor power, water, food, oxygen, and radiation shielding.
- 🌪️ **Dynamic Environmental Events** — Respond to unexpected mission events and operational hazards.
- 🎯 **Mission Objectives & Performance** — Complete objectives, make strategic decisions, and maintain a positive mission score.
- 🛡️ **Astronaut Safety System** — Manage suit oxygen and astronaut health while operating outside the designated safe zone.
- 🤖 **NOVA AI Mission Companion** — Receive mission guidance, survival information, and space science explanations.
- 🛰️ **Interactive Space Environments** — Explore 3D outpost environments and space-related assets.
- 📚 **Educational Space Simulation** — Learn about planetary environments, resource sustainability, life-support systems, and the challenges of living beyond Earth.

### ⚙️ Core Survival Resources

| Resource | Operational Purpose |
|---|---|
| ⚡ Power | Supports essential outpost systems and operations. |
| 💧 Water | Maintains the crew's water supply. |
| 🍎 Food | Supports crew nutrition and long-duration survival. |
| 🫁 Oxygen | Maintains breathable air and astronaut survival. |
| 🛡️ Radiation Shielding | Represents protection against hazardous space radiation. |

**Mission Success Conditions**

A successful campaign requires players to:
- Complete the full mission duration.
- Keep critical resources above zero.
- Complete all required mission objectives.
- Maintain a positive mission score.

A mission that fails to meet these conditions does not qualify for a successful mission certificate.

## 🧑‍🚀 04. How to Play

1. Select your destination: Moon or Mars.
2. Enter the planetary outpost as an astronaut.
3. Explore the environment and interact with mission locations.
4. Monitor the five essential survival resources.
5. Respond to daily mission events and operational challenges.
6. Make strategic decisions to protect the crew and maintain the outpost.
7. Complete mission objectives and survive the entire campaign.

## 🧭 07. Development Roadmap — From Web to Immersive Simulation

AstroBase is envisioned as an evolving space simulation platform. Our roadmap focuses on making space exploration accessible today while building toward more immersive and high-fidelity planetary experiences.

### 🌐 Phase 1 — Web Accessibility via Three.js

<kbd>CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION</kbd>

Our first phase focuses on delivering an accessible browser-based 3D simulation that users can experience without installing specialized gaming software.

**Core Development Goals:**
- Interactive 3D planetary outposts using Three.js and React Three Fiber.
- Third-person astronaut movement and exploration.
- Moon and Mars survival campaigns.
- Resource management and life-support-inspired systems.
- Dynamic mission events and decision-making.
- NOVA AI Mission Companion.
- Browser performance optimization and accessibility.

<kbd>DELIVERED FOUNDATION</kbd>

### 🪐 Phase 2 — High-Fidelity Simulation & VR via Unreal Engine 5

<kbd>FUTURE DEVELOPMENT VISION</kbd>

The next planned phase is to explore a transition toward Unreal Engine 5 to expand AstroBase into a more immersive, high-fidelity planetary simulation.

**Planned Development Directions:**
- More detailed lunar and Martian terrain environments.
- Advanced lighting, atmospheric effects, materials, and environmental rendering.
- More realistic astronaut, habitat, and spacecraft interactions.
- Advanced environmental and life-support simulations.
- Potential virtual reality (VR) exploration and mission experiences.
- Expanded AI-assisted mission guidance and immersive learning.

### 🎯 Long-Term Vision

Our goal is to evolve AstroBase beyond a browser-based survival game into an educational space simulation platform that helps users understand the engineering, environmental, and operational challenges of sustaining human life beyond Earth.

**From accessible web exploration to immersive planetary simulation — the mission continues.**
