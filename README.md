## 🌌 About AstroBase

**AstroBase** is an interactive Moon and Mars survival simulator developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

The project puts players in the role of astronauts responsible for operating and maintaining an extraterrestrial outpost. Players must manage essential resources, respond to unexpected mission events, complete objectives, and make strategic decisions to keep their crew and base operational.

With a third-person astronaut experience, interactive 3D environments, mission progression, and an AI-powered mission companion, AstroBase transforms space exploration concepts into an engaging survival experience.

## 🎮 Key Features

- 🚀 **Interactive 3D Gameplay** — Explore the mission environment through third-person astronaut movement.
- 🌕 **Moon Outpost Campaign** — Complete a 20-day lunar survival mission.
- 🔴 **Mars Outpost Campaign** — Survive a challenging 30-day Martian campaign.
- 🧑‍🚀 **Astronaut Exploration** — Move around the base and interact with mission environments.
- ⚡ **Resource Management** — Monitor and maintain power, water, food, oxygen, and radiation shielding.
- 🌪️ **Dynamic Mission Events** — Respond to mission challenges and make decisions that affect survival.
- 🎯 **Mission Objectives & Scoring** — Track progress, complete objectives, and evaluate mission performance.
- 🛡️ **Survival Zone System** — Manage suit oxygen and astronaut health while exploring outside the safe zone.
- 🤖 **NOVA AI Mission Companion** — Get mission-related guidance and space science information.
- 🛰️ **NASA-Inspired Assets & Learning** — Explore a space environment featuring NASA-related models and educational content.

## 🧑‍🚀 How to Play

1. Choose your mission environment: Moon or Mars.
2. Enter the 3D mission environment as an astronaut.
3. Explore your outpost and interact with mission locations.
4. Monitor your essential resources.
5. Respond to daily mission events by selecting appropriate decisions.
6. Complete mission objectives and maintain positive mission performance.
7. Survive the entire campaign to accomplish your mission.

## 🤖 Meet NOVA — Your Mission Companion

**NOVA** is the AI-powered mission companion designed to support players throughout their AstroBase journey.

NOVA helps players with:
- Mission-related questions and guidance.
- Moon and Mars survival information.
- Space science and NASA-related knowledge.
- Educational explanations about mission systems.

## 🛰️ Project Vision

AstroBase aims to make space exploration more interactive and accessible by combining game-based learning, resource management, 3D simulation, and AI-assisted educational experiences.

The long-term vision is to create a realistic and engaging environment where players can better understand the challenges of establishing and sustaining human settlements beyond Earth.
